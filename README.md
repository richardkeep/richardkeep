## What I do

I take products from a blank `laravel new` to a system real people depend on every day: payments, member portals, learning platforms, point-of-sale, internal tools, and the APIs and mobile apps that sit around them.

Most of my work lives where the stakes are real. I've built for professional bodies, universities, microfinance lenders, retailers, and government-adjacent teams, so I think about audit trails, reconciliation, and uptime from day one.

## Where I'm strongest

**🧱 The Laravel ecosystem, end to end**
Laravel has been my main tool for close to 100 projects. I know the whole ecosystem: Livewire, Volt and Flux for server-driven UIs, Inertia with Vue or React when a richer SPA makes sense, Nova and Filament for admin panels, and Horizon, Octane, Sanctum and Fortify behind them.

**💳 Payments & fintech**
I've integrated **M-Pesa (Daraja)**, card payments through **Cybersource**, and invoicing and reconciliation flows. Some of these systems run lending and microfinance operations. I build them to be idempotent, auditable, and boring in the best way.

**🏢 Business systems that run organisations**
I've shipped LMS platforms, membership and CPD tracking, procurement, recruitment, per-diem and claims workflows, POS and inventory, and multi-tenant SaaS. This is software with real roles, permissions, approvals, and reports.

**🎨 Clean, fast interfaces**
I build with Tailwind CSS, Vue and React, and I care a lot about polish. A good UI is often the difference between software people put up with and software they actually like using.

**🚀 Shipping & operations**
I handle Docker, zero-downtime deploys, queues and workers, MySQL replication and HA, backups, and monitoring myself. I own the work all the way to production and keep it healthy once it's there.

**✅ Quality by default**
I test with Pest, format with Pint, and keep CI green. I write code the next developer can pick up, and that developer is usually me six months later.
